    <strong>Heal chin, queue: Emma, Joe</strong>
  </p>

  <p>
    Hey fool, sit-com in mobbing, glare:<br />
    That hole, more tallow, so speed row.<br />
    Stepped the spoil year, him, ’em, more: “eh?”.<br />
    Or, bad it aunt, though spear raw.<br />
    Call sea, perk cause attorney tar,<br />
    Lather hull, noon’s your star.
  </p>

  <p>
    Moot happens sandal, loot team are,<br />
    Ore addle, worm fat alley.<br />
    Nay, sack one dough, nah, seem ill hell:<br />
    Home add hippy, ehm, more tally.<br />
    Lasso hack, crew hen tap, all-over ray,<br />
    A cull pace starve, her run.
  </p>

  <p>
    <small>
      Jeffacious Habrookly,<br />
      November 2013
    </small>
  </p>
